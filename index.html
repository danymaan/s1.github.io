About ShepherdOne

A practical partner for your data journey.

ShepherdOne LLC is an IT consulting business focused on helping organizations make better use of their data and technology. We bring together business analysis, data engineering, enterprise analytics, and delivery leadership to move initiatives from early discovery through implementation and production.

Our experience spans complex, data-intensive environments—including healthcare and property & casualty insurance—with capabilities that transfer across industries. We work closely with business stakeholders, architects, engineers, and delivery teams to create solutions that are reliable, scalable, and aligned to real business needs.

Business context meets technical execution.

We connect the “why” behind a business problem with the data, architecture, and delivery practices needed to solve it—without losing sight of quality, usability, or outcomes.
